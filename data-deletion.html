Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 2026

You can request deletion of your personal data and account information from the Invoicing App (invoice.digital1now.com) at any time.

How to Request Deletion

To request that we delete your account and associated personal data, email info@digital1now.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", including:

  • The email address associated with your account
  • Your business/organization name (if applicable)

What Happens Next

We will verify your request and delete your personal account data from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain records (such as invoicing or payroll data) to comply with tax or legal obligations. Data required for legal retention will be securely archived and not used for any other purpose.

Third-Party Login Data

If you signed in using Google or Facebook, deleting your account will also remove any profile information (name, email) obtained from that login provider from our systems. This does not affect your Google or Facebook account itself — to manage what our app can access via those providers, use your Google or Facebook account settings directly.

Contact Us

Questions about this process can be sent to info@digital1now.com.
