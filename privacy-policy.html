Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Invoicing App ("we", "our", "us"), operated by Digital 1 Now, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our invoicing and payroll platform at invoice.digital1now.com.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and business/organization details you provide when signing up or logging in (including via Google or Facebook login).
  • Invoicing and payroll data: customer records, invoices, quotes, purchase orders, and payroll information you enter into the platform.
  • Payment information: processed securely through our payment partner (Helcim); we do not store full card numbers ourselves.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the invoicing, payroll, and related features of the platform.
  • To authenticate your account (including through third-party login providers such as Google and Facebook).
  • To communicate with you about your account, invoices, or support requests.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the platform, including Supabase (database, authentication, and storage), Helcim (payment processing), and login providers such as Google and Facebook. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function.

Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal and tax record-keeping obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions page for details.
